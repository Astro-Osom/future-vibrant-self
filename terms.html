Terms & Privacy

Your story stays yours.

Last updated August 4, 2026

Future Vibrant Self turns your words and images into a personal documentary. Everything below is one promise, said in plain language: what you make here belongs to you.

Quality review

Our team privately reviews the films and story documents the app generates for you, solely to improve the product. Your recordings and films are never public, never shared outside the team, and never used in marketing without your explicit permission. Your story remains yours: if you delete a film — or your account — our internal review notes about it are deleted as well.

That last promise isn’t just words. Deletion is enforced by construction: removing your account or a film automatically removes the associated review notes from our systems.

Your recordings

  • Your video, audio, photos, and the story we help you shape are yours. We don’t claim ownership of your content.
  • We use your content only to generate your story document and film, and to privately improve the product as described above.
  • We don’t sell your content, and we don’t share it with third parties for advertising.
  • You can delete a film or your account at any time; when you do, we delete the associated content and our notes about it.

The waitlist

When you join the waitlist, we collect your name and email for one reason: to tell you when the beta opens and to share occasional news about Future Vibrant Self. We won’t sell your email or share it with anyone else, and you can ask us to remove you at any time.

Questions about any of this? Email futurevibrantself@gmail.com and a real person will answer.
