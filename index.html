Who Is Your Future Vibrant Self?

Watch the trailer

We carry a story that changed us

Heartbreak, loss, illness, reinvention, hitting rock bottom. We survived it, but struggle to articulate it and find the meaning. So the story stays mentally trapped. We don't have the tools, the framework, or the time to turn something deeply personal into something clear and cinematic.

  • “I don't know how to make a movie.”
  • “I don't know how to tell my story.”
  • “I don't know how to edit.”

But what if we could turn it into a custom movie?

FVS App

The word “question” does not exist
without the word “quest” inside it.

  • Future Vibrant Self is a mobile-first storytelling app
  • Guides you through a structured, question-based personal transformation quest
  • Turns a confusing or painful chapter into something clear, meaningful, and cinematic
  • Structures your answers into a finished, emotionally coherent cinematic documentary
  • A finished artifact you can keep, rewatch, share — a digital legacy
  • All you need is your phone

We're building the future of storytelling.

The Film

Adventures in Personal Transformation

After years of working on films like The Dark Knight Rises, Interstellar, and Captain America: Civil War, I turned the camera on myself and made the most personal film of my life. Future Vibrant Self is the framework that helped me navigate divorce, physical change, grief, and unhealed trauma — and emerge freer and more aligned. This is the same path I'm now opening for others who want to own their story and step into their vibrant self.
