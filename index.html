Where It Began

After grief, divorce, and the sudden loss of his career, founder Frank Reina turned to his own life as material. He made a movie about confronting what had happened, rebuilding himself, and becoming someone new.

That experience revealed a simple truth: when we give shape to the chapters that changed us, our stories can give someone else the courage to shape their own future.
